MTRX-1K - the firmware folder
=============================

MTRX-1K is an emulator: it contains no program and no sounds of the instrument.
Both come from the firmware of the instrument itself, which you provide as files.

Copy into this folder (Documents\GPR Music Project\MTRX-1K\ROM) your own copy of:

  - the 32 KB system EPROM of the instrument, firmware version 1.20   (required)
  - the 64 KB patch EPROM, for the factory patches of banks 2 to 9    (optional)

Both are plain binary images with the extension .bin. They are recognised by
their size and content, so their names do not matter.

Then start MTRX-1K, or press Power on its panel if it is already open.

The user manual explains the rest: press Manual on the panel of MTRX-1K, or open
"MTRX-1K User Manual" from the Start menu.
